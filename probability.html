Where is the 0.75? It is not a region of the diagram. Every region is a share of all adults; 0.75 is a share of the S circle: the overlap divided by the whole S circle, 0.3225 / 0.43 = 0.75. Notice the C circle sits almost entirely inside S: 0.3225 / 0.37 ≈ 87% of adults with some college own stock.
